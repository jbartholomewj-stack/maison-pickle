Comforting Classics

A classic eatery serving iconic, comforting American favourites. The French dip is the signature, the pastry department builds the cakes in-house, and the bar is made for lingering.

In 2016 we revived a landmark Broadway space, once home to Ouest, an Upper West Side fine-dining pioneer loved by artists, writers and performers. We kept the glamour and added the soul of an American diner. We're the sister restaurant to Jacob's Pickles.

    Know Before You Go

    Tips straight from guest reviews, so your visit goes smoothly.

    Do I need a reservation?

    We recommend one, especially for weekend brunch and Friday nights. Walk-ins can wait up to an hour at peak times. Booking is easy on Resy, and guests say tables are “not hard to secure.”

    When is it quieter?

    Weekday lunch, right when we open at 11am, is our calmest time. Weekend brunch and dinner are lively, with a great playlist that can get loud. For a conversation, ask for a mezzanine or patio table.

    How much should we order?

    Less than you think! Most plates feed two. Start with one bread for every three or four people, share your mains and save room for cake. Try the Share Planner.

    Can I bring my dog or the kids?

    Families are always welcome, and guests love bringing their pups to the side patio, away from the bustle of Amsterdam Ave. Service dogs are welcome everywhere.

    Anything else?

    Bring a light layer, because the dining room runs cool on hot days. Bar seats often open up faster than tables. We serve alcohol from 10am at weekend brunch.
